Abodeo privacy

Abodeo is a small demonstration app for organising home projects. You can use it without signing in. This page explains what is kept about you if you choose to sign in with Google.

What is kept

  • The permanent ID that Google gives your Google account.
  • Your name and email address, as Google gave them the first time you signed in. They are not updated if you change them at Google later.
  • A preferred name, if you choose to set one. Only you can see it.
  • A sign-in record for each browser you sign in from, so that you stay signed in. It ends when you sign out.
  • The ID of the Abodeo calendar in your Google Calendar, if you have used Google Calendar with Abodeo as described below.
  • A record of sessions with their lengths: when you signed in, and when you used Abodeo and for how long, worked out from when you click or type in it. It is kept until your account is deleted.
  • A label, if one has been set in the browser you use, such as a first name. It is kept with your sign-in and session records so that the person running Abodeo can tell who used an account. Anyone can type a label into a browser, so it is not checked, it is not used to decide what anyone can see or do, and it is not shown to other users.
  • The projects and tasks you add or change while signed in, so that you can use them on every device where you sign in, and those you choose to move in from your browser.
  • The images you add to your projects while signed in, with their captions and the time you added them. An image is kept exactly as you added it, including any location or other details inside the file, and the server also keeps a small thumbnail of it. Only your account can see your images: there is no public address for them. Images cannot be added while signed out.

Abodeo does not keep your Google sign-in tokens, your profile photo, your IP address or details of your browser. Signing in asks Google only for your basic profile and email address.

Google Calendar

Once signed in, you can add appointments to your projects, which Abodeo puts in Google Calendar, and you can create a test appointment there. Only then does Abodeo ask Google for access to your calendar, and Google asks for your permission.

  • The permission lets Abodeo make its own calendars in your Google account, and see, create, change and delete the events in them. It does not let Abodeo see or change your other calendars or their events.
  • Abodeo makes one calendar, named Abodeo, and adds your appointments and test appointments to it. It keeps the calendar's ID, so that the same calendar is used each time.
  • An appointment is an event in your Abodeo calendar. Abodeo keeps no copy of it: it shows a project's appointments by reading them from Google Calendar. Each event carries the ID of its project, in a private property that only Abodeo can read, so that Abodeo knows which project it belongs to, and holds the title, times and notes you gave it. Deleting your Abodeo account does not delete these events, as they are in your own Google Calendar: delete them there.
  • Google gives Abodeo a token to use your calendar. Abodeo keeps it for up to an hour, or until Google ends it if sooner, in an encrypted cookie in your browser that only Abodeo's own pages and not other sites can send back to it. It is removed when you sign out, and it is never saved in Abodeo's database, so Google may ask again when it has ended.
  • You can withdraw the permission at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The Abodeo calendar and its appointments stay in your Google Calendar until you delete them there.

Why

To recognise you when you sign in again, from any browser, to show you your name, preferred name and projects, and to see when and how much Abodeo is used while it is being tried out. Nothing is shared with anyone, and nothing is used for advertising.

What is not linked to you

Projects and tasks you add while signed out stay in your own browser and are not sent to Abodeo's server, unless you choose to move them into your account when you sign in. While you are signed in, your projects are kept on the server, not in your browser, and signing out leaves none of them in the browser. Your browser's settings, such as how projects are shown, stay in the browser.

How long it is kept, and how to remove it

What is listed above is kept until it is removed. To have it removed, email the support address shown on Google's sign-in screen for Abodeo, from the email address you signed in with. Everything kept about you is then deleted, including your projects and tasks, your images and captions, your Abodeo calendar's ID and the record of your sessions.

Where it is kept

In a Postgres database run by Neon, used by the app's server, which is hosted by Vercel.
