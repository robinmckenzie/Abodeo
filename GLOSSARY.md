# Glossary

**Demo**:
The experience a prospective employer gets from the link in Robin's CV: signed in automatically as the demo account, then taken through the tour.

**Demo account**:
The shared abodeodemo@gmail.com account that every demo visitor uses.

**Tour**:
The walkthrough of Abodeo's parts, shown as an overlay with speech bubbles over the real screens.

**Usage label**:
A short self-declared name a visitor carries in the address (`?label=NAME`), stored with every usage record so that Robin can tell who used an account.
